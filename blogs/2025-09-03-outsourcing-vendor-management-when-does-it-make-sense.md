Vendor issues are increasingly difficult to navigate as data breaches, operational disruptions, and emerging risks continue to change at a rapid pace. On top of that, your organization must continue activities like due diligence, risk assessments, and continuous monitoring. By outsourcing vendor management, your organization can minimize costs, improve risk management outcomes, and reap the consequential benefits.
